              Speaker turns — &sN-start ... &sN-end

              Press & to open the speaker picker.

                  Annotation Shortcuts

                  [ ] — Mispronunciation: [verbatim](normalized)

                  [सक्रीन](स्क्रीन)
                  [iskool](school)

                  ! — Code-Mix: !lang[verbatim](normalized). Language optional.

                  !en[ट्रेन](train)
                  ![लोकेशन](location)

                  # — Named Entity: #TYPE[verbatim](normalized). Type optional.

                  #PER[रमेश](रमेश)
                  #GPE[नई दिल्ली](नई दिल्ली)
                  #DATETIME[उन्नीस सौ पचासी](1985)

                  $ — Accent: $accent-name[verbatim](normalized). Name optional.

                  $british[wa'er](water)
                  $telugu[iskool](school)
                  $[नईं](नहीं)

                  @ — Isolated tokens & span pairs

                  Hesitations: @umm, @uhh, @ehh…
                  Sounds: @laughter, @cough, @breathe…
                  Other: @silence, @stutter-block, @unintelligible
                  Disfluency spans: @filler-start … @filler-end, @repair-start reparandum - repair @repair-end
                  Paralinguistic spans: @laughing-start … @laughing-end
                  Prosody: @emphasis-start … @emphasis-end, @raising-pitch-start … @raising-pitch-end, @falling-pitch-start … @falling-pitch-end

                  & — Speaker Turn: &sN-start … &sN-end

                  &s1-start नमस्ते &s1-end
                  &s2-start कैसे हो? &s2-end

                  The type/name after a prefix is optional — #[…](…), $[…](…), ![…](…) all render as their category with an "unspecified" label.
