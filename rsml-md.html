OpenBhasha Annotation UX

Guidelines
Noise Tagging

Use @ for noise tags.

Examples: @noise, @background-laughter

Code-Mixing

[native](mixed)

Examples: [ट्रेन](train), [स्टडी](study)

Entities

{verbatim}(normalized)

Multi-word entities: {भारतीय रयिलवे}(इंडियन रेलवे), {स्टेट बैंक ऑफ इंडिया}(एस्बीआई), {नई दिल्ली}(नई ढिल्ली)

Accent/Mispronunciation

<verbatim>(normalized)

Examples: <हमाये>(हमारे), <समझनाहीं>(समझ नहीं)
